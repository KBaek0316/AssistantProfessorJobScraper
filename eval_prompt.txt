Analyze the following academic job posting and evaluate its relevance against the candidate's research profile.

{candidate_profile}

=== JOB POSTING TO EVALUATE ===
Title (scraped): {title}
Institution (scraped): {institution}

Job Description / Text Snippet:
"""{raw_description}"""

=== EVALUATION INSTRUCTIONS ===
1. Determine if this is an academic faculty role (Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Full Professor, Open Rank Faculty, or Lecturer/Instructor). Set "is_faculty" to false if this is a postdoc, research scientist/fellow without faculty status, driver, technician, staff, or K-12 role.
2. Evaluate how closely the job matches the candidate's research profile on a 1 to 10 scale:
   - 9-10 (Core Match): The position explicitly seeks expertise in Transportation Engineering, Transit Systems, Travel Behavior Analysis, Discrete Choice Modeling, Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS), or AI/Data Science applied to transportation/mobility.
   - 7-8 (Strong Interdisciplinary Match): The position is in Urban Planning, Systems Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or Computing/Data Science, but specifically focuses on transportation, mobility systems, smart cities, or urban analytics.
   - 4-6 (Moderate / Borderline Fit): Broad Civil & Environmental Engineering or Systems Engineering search where transportation is one of several acceptable sub-disciplines, or an open-topic data science/computing faculty search.
   - 1-3 (Low Relevance / Irrelevant): The role is in an unrelated field (e.g. English, Film, Nursing, Pharmacy, Education) OR is in a non-matching civil engineering subfield (e.g. Water Resources/Hydrology, Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Environmental Chemistry) even if the description mentions a state Department of Transportation (e.g. TxDOT) merely as a potential funding source, partner, or advisory body.
3. Provide a concise, 1-sentence "fit_reason" summarizing why this score was assigned (e.g., "Core match: focuses on transit planning and travel behavior analysis" or "Low fit: water resources management position; incidental mention of TX DOT co-work opportunity only").
