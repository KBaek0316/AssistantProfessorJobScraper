=== EVALUATION INSTRUCTIONS ===
1. Determine Position Eligibility & Seniority:
   - Target positions: Tenure-track Assistant Professor, Open Rank Faculty searches that explicitly include Assistant Professor (e.g., "Assistant/Associate Professor" or "Open Rank"), and career-track university faculty appointments ("Assistant Professor of Teaching" at UCLA or "Lecturer in Discipline" at Columbia University).
   - EXCLUDE (set "is_faculty" to false or assign fit_score: 1 with an explanatory fit_reason):
     * Seniority-only roles: Pure Associate Professor (without Assistant), Full Professor, Department Chair, Division Chair, Dean, Endowed Chair (e.g. "Professor & Department Chair" at University of Pittsburgh).
     * Contingent / part-time / non-degree roles: Adjunct Faculty/Professor (e.g. Morgan State University Adjunct), Lecturer in Continuing Education, Adult Education, Extension (e.g. City Colleges of Chicago Continuing Education). Note: "Lecturer in Discipline" (Columbia University) and "Assistant Professor of Teaching" (UCLA) are formal university faculty lines and must NOT be screened out as contingent.
     * Trainees and non-faculty staff: Postdocs, research fellows, technicians, staff, drivers.

2. Evaluate Geographic Scope:
   - Target Regions: United States (US), Canada, European countries (e.g. United Kingdom, Germany, France, Netherlands, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Italy, Spain, Belgium, Austria, Ireland, Poland, Portugal, etc.), Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan.
   - EXCLUDE: Mainland China (PRC, Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, etc. - explicitly DO NOT exclude Hong Kong or Taiwan) and non-target countries (e.g. Middle East, Latin America, South Asia, Africa).
   - If outside target scope, set "is_faculty" to false or assign fit_score: 1 with fit_reason: "Screened out: Location outside target geographic scope ([Location])".

3. Calibrated Candidate Fit Score (1 to 10 scale):
   Evaluate the posting against the candidate's Core Field, Target Departments, and Research Specialties in the CANDIDATE PROFILE:
   - 9-10 (Core Match): Dedicated Assistant Professor position directly matching the candidate's primary field and core research specialties (e.g., searches specifically seeking expertise in the candidate's primary subdiscipline or core research topics).
   - 7-8 (Strong Interdisciplinary Match): Assistant Professor or career-track faculty in related disciplines or departments (listed under Target Departments) that explicitly seek applications to the candidate's core domain, research methods, or interdisciplinary specialties.
   - 6-7 (Multi-Track / Broad Fit): Broad or open-subdiscipline searches within the candidate's primary department where the candidate's subfield is explicitly listed as one of several acceptable focus areas.
   - 3-4 (Low / Poor Fit):
     * General department faculty searches that do NOT explicitly mention or prioritize the candidate's subfield or research specialties.
     * Peripheral departments or business/policy schools with only incidental overlap.
   - 1-2 (Screened Out / Irrelevant):
     * Non-matching subdisciplines listed under 'Irrelevant / Non-Matching Subfields' that have NO component of the candidate's core expertise — even if incidental external agencies, partners, or data sources are mentioned.
     * Positions seeking senior-only appointments, adjuncts, continuing education, or completely unrelated fields (Nursing, Film, Pharmacy, K-12, etc.).

4. Department & Focus Extraction:
   - Diligently extract the official Department, School, or Division name from the posting body (e.g., "Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering", "Department of Industrial Engineering", etc.). NEVER leave department as "Not specified" if any department, school, college, or subject focus is mentioned in the text.

5. Provide a concise, 1-sentence "fit_reason" explaining the score.
